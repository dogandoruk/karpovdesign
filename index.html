Architectural Philosophy

The Design Mindset: From Idea to Building

Clients hire a mind and a disciplined process. Every project progresses through five transparent evolutionary stages.

01

Hand Sketching

First gestural ink studies exploring sightlines, solar angles, and natural land contours.

02

Basswood Models

Physical study models crafted by hand to test physical light penetration and volume relationships.

03

3D Virtual BIM

Revit LOD 350 coordination resolving clashes and structural tolerances before groundbreaking.

04

Site Execution

Job-site visits verifying timber framing, concrete curing, and architectural reveals.

05

The Lived Space

The resolved atmosphere bathed in natural daylight, tailored around how the client truly lives.

IZKA Headquarters (İzmir Chamber of Commerce)

The Process Strip (Sketches, Study Models & Parametric Detailing) P01 – P04

The Architectural Challenge

How does an institutional chamber of commerce open itself up to the civic public while delivering high-security municipal auditoriums and environmental climate resilience?

The project redefines the ground plane as an extension of the Mediterranean boulevard, pulling pedestrians into a monumental 5-story atrium organized around a spiraling public exhibition ramp.
