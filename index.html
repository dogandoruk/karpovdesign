İZKA Headquarters (İzmir Development Agency)

The Architectural Challenge

As detailed in the 2016 portfolio (Pages 30–31), the İZKA Headquarters anchors the waterfront development boulevard in İzmir, establishing an open public ground plane that pulls citizens directly into a soaring five-story atrium.

Aleksandr Karpov contributed directly to the design development, 3D modeling, BIM coordination, and technical visualization of the winning competition entry that went on to receive the prestigious Turgut Cansever National Architecture Award.
