Commercial / Mixed-Use

İZKA Headquarters

Designed as a civic landmark for regional economic development, the headquarters synthesizes high-efficiency office environments with open public galleries and vertical gardens.

The central atrium operates as a passive thermal chimney, inducing natural ventilation across the core while the outer double-skin curtain wall attenuates solar heat gain in the Mediterranean climate.
