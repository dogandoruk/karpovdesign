Architecture is a tapestry of systems.
  
	
	
	
	
It intertwines the tangible and ethereal, creating . 
The unavoidable, yet open ended settings for our lives.  	
	
It is absolute, yet fluid.  
	
	
The act of architectural design is a process deeply rooted in the physical reality 
and yet it has no absolute future as our surroundings continuously change.    
	
	
They are changed by us and they change us becoming an integral and permanent player in the human experiment.  
	
This becomes a feedback loop with ripples going through time, touching all generations, 
cultures, climates and ideologies.  
	
This tapestry  is ever expanding with the reach of human presence encapsulating our reality and potential.  
Architecture is the very manifestation of the human condition as we terraform our environment not only to meet our needs, 
    but also to satisfy the desires, the curiosities, the wants.  
	
	
	
	
	
It is an yearning for the answer, to the  constant question 
what if   .    .      .

  And let it be fruitful.

out to make interior feel larger, wrapping around the guest.

Sivas Unity Bridge
DATE:	
2016
TYPE:	
National Competition
USE:	
Bridge/ Park-scape
SIZE:	
150 m span, 120,000 m2
PLACE:	
Sivas (Turkey)
STATUS:	 Proposal
TEAM:	
Aboutblank
The width of the river is 100 m.  The bridge is expanded to accommodate the vehicular and pedestrian requirements while 
opening the bridge to the river and park.  An airy sculpture forms the bridges uninterrupted span. Park paths are integrated 
into a pedestrian ring road by the water.  Seasonal overflow zones in the park frame garden wetlands at each end of the bridge.
The bridge connects the main city to the university complex through a riverside 
park.  The flatland inspired a longer drawn out path for the tram and car bridge.
Pockets of social spaces are formed within park-lane and bridge intersections.
A traditional solid bridge is split into smaller segments for a lighter structure and lessened impact on the 
park.  Pedestrian path is brought closer to the water and is complimented by the structure beams above.

An existing vehicle depot is to be reused as a cultural hub, 
expanding the functions and dorm space of the nearby univer-
sity and adding a new commercially viable zone for the local 
neighborhood.
The first stage involved a blank slate ideas competition, the sec-
ond stage introduced an expanded residential program. 
Most of the warehouse structure was not fit to keep, but the 
main rhythm of the loading bays could be used in the project.


=== PAGE 33 ===
The final master plan retained the diverse function banding from the first stage, and had an added element of an elevated 
courtyard dormitory housing concept above tying it together.  The individual bars become linked  in the housing courtyard.
THE CONVENTION CENTER: Linking Sections
Minimal intervention left the primary warehouse in an 
ideal state for diverse event functions and the overhead 
gate linked it to the museum. Resulting combinations offer 
a convenient venue for a variety of public events.
Functions were rearranged based on use proximity to the conference center.  Each zone has a unique diagram infusing its 
function with a tectonic identity.  The iconography of the original scheme becomes legible from the ground level.  The complex 
itself is an extension of the park and is fully permeable.  The development is meant to be used in close collaboration with the 
university and hold functions and events for the city, neighborhood and school.
